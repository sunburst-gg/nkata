Viewer role · v1

Viewers see the whole game and leave notes. Nothing else is on screen.

For investors, publishers, outside writers, voice directors. No Blender, no Unreal, no folder. They check in once a week. Every action a viewer cannot take is removed, not disabled. Each frame below is the live prototype at that state.
