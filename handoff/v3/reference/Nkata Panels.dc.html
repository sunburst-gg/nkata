Rules
OrderHeader, mirror, reference, checks, comments, Publish, presence. Unreal inserts Review after checks and Level parts after Review. Mirror status44 px row, 12 px dot, 16 px label, time in mono. One line on failure. The panel is often out of focus, so the dot and the word carry it alone. PublishThe only accent fill in the panel. Disabled reads as a reason, never as grey alone: "2 checks block", "Ready. Waiting on Emeka." WordsIn Unreal, in Blender, Publish, Mark Ready, Approve, Send back. Never a path, a hash, an export, or an import. MotionDot color and label 150 ms cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1). Syncing pulses 1.2 s. Published confirmation holds 1.5 s, then returns to Publish.
